Nothing you load ever leaves your machine

Both tools run entirely in the browser. The match video, the session PDF and every drawing are read from your own disk and stay there — there is no server, no account, and no upload.

The flip side: closing the tab loses your work. Use Save in the tagger before you close, and Load to pick up where you left off.
